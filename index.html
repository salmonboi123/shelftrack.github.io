ShelfTrack

An automated pantry inventory system powered by Raspberry Pi.

Mission Team Repository

Our Mission

ShelfTrack is designed to eliminate kitchen waste and streamline grocery management through automated hardware. By integrating computer vision and standard barcode scanning via a Raspberry Pi, we provide a seamless, low-power solution to track pantry inventory in real-time.

Whether you're scanning items in after a grocery run or relying on the camera module to monitor shelf capacity, our system ensures you always know exactly what you have in stock.
