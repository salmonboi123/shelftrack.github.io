Smarter Pantry Management.

An open-source, automated inventory system powered by Raspberry Pi. Track your pantry items seamlessly using integrated camera vision and barcode scanning.

Get Started Learn More

Built for the Modern Kitchen

Hardware and software combined to eliminate the guesswork from grocery shopping and meal planning.

Raspberry Pi Powered

Designed to run efficiently on Raspberry Pi hardware, acting as the central hub for local processing and inventory management.

Barcode Scanning

Quickly log items in and out of your pantry with a physical barcode scanner for rapid, error-free data entry.

Camera Integration

Visual tracking capabilities using an integrated camera to monitor shelf space and identify untagged produce.
